How do you play?

To play, you'll need to go to the 'Play' page of the website. There you'll see three options, which choose the difficulty: Casual (the song name is mentioned in the clip), Familiar (the song clip will have lyrics), and Worshiper (the song clip won't have lyrics, or will only contain a few).

Next, you'll need to pick how many songs you want to guess in the game. The options are Singularity (1), Trinity (3), and Quintessence (5).

After that, you'll be in the game, guessing the song. At first, what you see can be confusing, but it's really simple once you know what everything does. The text at the top is just the difficulty level; the three 'X's are your strikes (how many wrong guesses you can make); and below the strikes are the song indicators. These tell you not only how many songs you have to guess but also whether you correctly guessed a song, skipped it, got it wrong, or whether it's the song you're currently guessing. The sound controls are below the song indicators and have four actions: Play/Pause, Replay/Repeat Song, Skip Song, and Submit Guess. Below that is the song progress bar, which shows how much time is left on the song. Lastly is the search bar/drop-down menu: if you click the search bar, a drop-down menu will appear with all the songs.

Now that you understand what you see, you'll need to actual guess a song. There are around 113 song from 47 artists in the rotation (there is a chance I add more). The entire game is listening to the audio clip and trying the guess the song, the difficulty increasing from Casual to Worshiper.

What are the 'Song Indicators'?

The song indicators tells you the state of what the song is in. It also tell you the amount of song you need to guess. There are 5 indicators: Blue - Song selected/you're on, Green - Guessed correct, Orange - Skipped, Grey/very dark blue - Song Unseen and a Hidden Red colour which you can't see because it puts up the end game screen.

What are Strikes?

Strikes are the amount of mistakes or wrong guesses you can make before you lose. Once all 3 of the strikes turn red, the game ends and you'll go to the end game screen to see how well you did. Strikes stay consistent at 3, so even if you play a game with 5 songs, you will always have 3 strikes.

What songs are there?

All the songs in rotation can be seen with the links in the 'Song List' page. The songs are based of the songs in my spotify playlist which I have gathered over the years.
